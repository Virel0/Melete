### Added

- A watch party says what last changed and who did it (`last_change`: play, pause, a jump or
  the next file, by whom and when), and each wait says when it began, when the party stops
  waiting, and whether it's for you (`waits`), so the player can say "Paused by …" or
  "Waiting for … to catch up". A guest is told "someone from the household" for a household
  member, as before.
