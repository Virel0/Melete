### Changed

- Buttons in the web UI come in levels: the one main action is filled with the accent,
  secondary actions have a tonal fill instead of an outline, and tertiary actions are words with
  an icon in front. All buttons are pill-shaped.
- A title's page: Play is always the accent colour (the title's own colour stays in the page's
  fade), with the time left or the length after its verb in lighter type. From the start is a
  secondary button; Mark watched, My List, Play trailer, Extras, Cast and crew and the
  collection are tertiary.
- A series' episodes: the season is chosen from a select, with how many episodes it has and how
  many are watched beside it. Each episode shows how far through it is as a bar on its still,
  its number before its name, and Mark watched as a round toggle at the end of its row; the
  episode partway through sits on a fill.
