### Added

- A Features page under System, beside Outside services, for Melete's own features that send
  nothing outside the server: library scans, Make room, intros and credits, seek-bar previews,
  offline copies and Auto DJ. It's laid out like Outside services, and the palette opens it.
- The palette opens each outside service's and feature's own page by its name.

### Changed

- Outside services opens on a short list: how many are on, then each service's name, what
  it's for and its state (On, Off, Locked by .env, or what it needs, such as an address),
  grouped by what they're for: Metadata, Lyrics, Downloading, Sign-in, Alerts, Watch parties
  and AI. Each opens a page of its own with its settings, Save and Test, the features that
  use it, and what leaves the server folded under its name. Links about one service, such as
  System status' TMDB findings and the Indexers page's proxy note, open its page.
- The services API answer gives each service's group, the page it's set up on and a few words
  on what it's for, and comes group by group.
