### Changed

- Watch's tiles let the art carry them: one line under each (the title, then the time left or
  its badge), the episode's code and how far through drawn on the art, and Go to series over
  the art's corner on hover, keyboard focus or a long press instead of a link under every
  tile. Tiles no longer lift on hover; the focus ring goes round the art.
- A series partway through shows once on Watch's home: in Continue watching, not again in Next
  up with another episode.
- Album cards show their name and artist only, and their Play, heart and More over the cover
  on hover, keyboard focus or a long press, rather than on every card at once; a liked album's
  heart stays.
- Titles, albums, playlists and pins without a picture get art drawn from their name (its
  initials on a colour of its own) instead of a blank square.
