### Changed

- The web UI tells its surfaces apart by their fill instead of 1 px lines: cards, chips, badges,
  the header, the side switch, menus, the sidebar and the music bar have no outlines. Inputs
  keep theirs, as do the lines that mean something (under tabs, between groups in a menu).
- A chosen chip, tab or view (Posters or Table) is filled with the text's colour, as the side
  switch's current side will be.
