### Added

- Movie releases named as converted to a high frame rate (`48`, `50`, `60` or `120 fps`,
  `interpolated`) are turned down, since their made-up frames can stutter on some players.
  Settings → Outside services → Deluge has a switch to take them anyway. Films really made at a
  high frame rate (the Hobbit films, Gemini Man, Billy Lynn's Long Halftime Walk) and series
  aren't affected.
