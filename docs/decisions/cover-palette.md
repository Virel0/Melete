Decision: A cover's colours also carry its palette: its main colours, up to five, each at least
3% of the cover and at least 64 apart in RGB, the most of it first. Colours worked out before
are worked out again once; until then the palette is empty. Melete makes no tall or full-screen
art: an app draws a full-screen player from the square cover and its colours.

Why: Full-screen players either show the art full width fading into its colours or the cover
over a blurred copy of itself; both are drawn by the app, and a gradient from the cover's own
colours needs more than the one background colour. A made video with the layout in it would
rarely match the app's.
