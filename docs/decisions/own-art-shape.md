Decision: A title's own poster and backdrop are served cropped to their kind's shape (2:3 and
16:9), as large as fits, centred on the picture's subject as the backdrop focus finds it (the
middle when it can't), never enlarged. Their artwork names include how they're made, so the new
crops get new URLs at the next scan.

Why: Answers promise a poster and a backdrop, and an app draws them in tiles of those shapes; a
square `folder.jpg` or a very wide fanart came out stretched or with bars. Apps keep artwork
forever by its URL, so a changed way of making it needs a new name.
