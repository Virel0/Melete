Decision: An anime episode whose file has no ASS/SSA track and no ASS/SSA file beside it is
noted, per series, as without styled subtitles. Finding a replacement is a search an admin
starts and chooses from, turning down the group of the copy in the library; nothing is grabbed
or replaced on its own, and searches otherwise don't prefer or require ASS.

Why: Most anime releases carry their subtitles as an ASS track, drawn with their signs and
styling; a copy without one shows plain words or none. A release's name doesn't say whether it
carries ASS, so the group that made the copy without them is the one clue a search can act on,
and an admin's choice is needed to tell the rest apart.
