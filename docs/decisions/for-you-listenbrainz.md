Decision: For You gives each listener songs from their own play history, some in the library to play and some not, to request, from ListenBrainz, while an admin has it on (`MELETE_LISTENBRAINZ`, off by default, with MusicBrainz on; Settings → Outside services → MusicBrainz). Its seeds are the 10 recordings and 10 artists (each track's first credited) a listener finished most in the last 90 days, of the tracks they may see, less those they flagged not interested, each weighed by its finished plays against the most of its kind. ListenBrainz Labs (labs.api.listenbrainz.org) is asked for the recordings listened to alongside each seed recording and the artists alongside each seed artist; the closest 25 recordings and 10 artists of each answer are taken, each scored by ListenBrainz's score against the best in its answer, times its seed's weight. ListenBrainz's API (api.listenbrainz.org) is asked for the most listened-to recordings of the 10 closest artists alongside; the first 10 of each are suggestions too, scored by their artist's score times their listens against that artist's most listened-to. An artist ListenBrainz refuses them for (it has at times answered 401 asking for an account's token) is left out of that list, not kept, and asked again next time. It's asked for the artists and album of the suggested recordings the library hasn't by id, 50 a request. A suggestion is one to play when a present track the listener may see has its recording id (from its tags or matching), or one of its artists' ids and the same title (folded as searches fold it); tracks by the artists alongside are ones to play too (5 of each considered, by id), but never one heard in the last 30 days, finished three times, flagged, or a seed's own recording; one the library has but that's left out isn't offered to request either. The rest are to request, each song once by its artist and title, marked as request search marks songs (wanted, or the listener's own request not declined). The list holds at most two by one artist (by their first credited artist's MusicBrainz id when it's known, else by name, so one artist credited two ways counts once) and one from one album, 40 in all: each kind gets up to 20 first, the rest go to the best of either; ties are broken on the track id or the recording id. Each song says which seed it came from. Lists are kept and served at once (`GET /api/v1/music/for-you`, the listener's own only), and made in the background (the `for-you` task, hourly and when someone's is due, 5 lists a run): the first time, then again once it's a day old and the listener's plays changed; a failed one keeps the list from before and is tried again after an hour. Each time it's served, what's no longer there for the listener (a track they can't see, heard or flagged since; a song now in the library for them) is left out. ListenBrainz's answers are kept a week by the id asked about. Only MusicBrainz ids leave the server: the seed recordings' and artists', the closest artists alongside (for their most listened-to recordings), and the suggested recordings' (for their artists and album); never a name, an account, play counts, titles or anything else about the household, and no ListenBrainz account or token. Requests go over https to those two hosts only, with Melete's User-Agent, one a second for all of them together, holding back for a 429's or 503's `Retry-After` and while `X-RateLimit-Remaining` is 0 for `X-RateLimit-Reset-In`; a hold over a minute fails the request. ListenBrainz goes through the proxy when its switch is on, as the other services do. A song to request's cover is its album's, offered as a search's are (only with covers of music not in the library on, listed for that listener alone). Turned off (or MusicBrainz off), there's no list, and every list and kept answer is forgotten when the services' setup is next made (at once on saving).

Why: Discover and the mixes only reorder what the library has; For You is the one place that suggests music the household hasn't got, so it needs listening data from outside, and ListenBrainz's is open, needs no account, and is asked by MusicBrainz ids, which Melete already has and which say nothing about who listens. Ten seeds of each kind, and ten artists alongside, cover what someone plays lately while keeping a first list to some 35 requests at one a second; the week's cache makes a remade list cost little. Leaving out what was heard in the last 30 days or finished three times keeps it discovery rather than Heavy Rotation, and two per artist and one per album keep one favourite from filling it. The most listened-to recordings of the artists alongside give songs to request by them, which their tracks in the library can't; ListenBrainz answered that call 401 asking for a token for a while on 2026-11-09, then answered it without one, and a token would tie the household to an account, so a refusal only leaves that artist's out. Labs' similar recordings don't say which artists' ids a recording has, so the API's recording metadata is asked for those, also only by id, so a song the library has under another recording id is still recognised and a song to request carries its album for its cover. Labs' algorithm names aren't a stable API, so each is one named constant.
