Decision: Every downloaded trailer's sound is levelled once, by Melete with its own ffmpeg: `loudnorm` measures it, then its linear second pass moves the whole trailer by one steady amount to −24 LUFS integrated, peaks under −2 dBTP and a loudness range of 11 LU, the picture copied as it is and the sound written again as AAC at 256 kb/s, beside the file and then put in its place. One within 1 LU of the target with its peaks under it, or with no sound, is left as it is; one ffmpeg can't level is kept as it was, with why, and not tried again. A trailer downloaded again is levelled again. Up to four a round of the trailer worker, newest first, only while nothing is being converted for someone watching, and for trailers kept from before as well as new ones, whether or not downloads are on.

Why: Trailers are mastered loud, and how loud changes with their age and studio, so one could be 10 dB or more louder than the film after it or the trailer before it. −24 LUFS is about where films themselves sit, so a trailer before a film is no louder than it. One linear move keeps the trailer's own dynamics; levelling it continuously, as the player's Levelled sound does, would pump. Doing it in Melete, rather than in the downloader, levels the trailers already downloaded with the same code.
