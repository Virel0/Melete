Decision: The web UI's Now Playing has a full-screen mode, coloured from the album's cover
colours (the background, text1 for words and text2 only for the large lyric lines not being
sung). Motion covers play muted and looped, with no controls, on the album's page and in Now
Playing only, never on grids of albums, and never when the device asks for reduced motion.

Why: The cover colours already meet contrast on their background, so the full-screen view can
take its look from the album without new checks; text2 reads at 3:1, enough only for large text.
A grid of looping videos would cost every visitor bandwidth and attention for little, and moving
pictures are exactly what reduced motion asks to leave out.
