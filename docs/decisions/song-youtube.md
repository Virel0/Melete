Decision: With `MELETE_SONG_YOUTUBE` on (off by default) and the trailer downloader running, a wanted song is fetched from YouTube once Soulseek has been searched for it (in the searches kept, 30 days) and nothing is on its way or in the library: Soulseek had nothing that fits, or every Soulseek download of it failed, ten minutes after the last of those. Songs wanted before it was turned on count the same, the newest first, one at a time. The downloader searches YouTube for the song's artist and title and gives Melete the first ten results; Melete takes one only when its title holds the song's (without what follows " - ", a featured artist, bracketed parts or a leading article), the artist is named in its title or is its channel (their whole name, or the first of several credited when it's more than a short word; accents, and "&", "y" and "et", read alike), it's within ten seconds of the song's length (no longer than 15 minutes when that isn't known), and nothing in its title makes it another version the song's doesn't ask for (cover, remix, live, sped up, slowed, karaoke and the like); the artist's own "- Topic" channel first, then their channel, then the nearest in length. Its audio is fetched as it comes (Opus or AAC, never converted), checked again to be the song's length, put beside the song's album or in `Singles` as a Soulseek song is, never replacing a file, and tagged as the song. No fitting video is believed for 30 days; a failure, or a job the downloader hasn't answered in a day, is tried again a day later, three times at most. The song's progress says what was tried: Soulseek searched with nothing that fits, looking on YouTube, downloading from YouTube, in the library from YouTube, or not on YouTube either and why. What leaves the server: the song's artist and title (YouTube's search) and the chosen video's id, only through the VPN.

Why: Some songs exist only as uploads on YouTube, and Soulseek searches for them will never find anything. Requiring title, artist and length to fit, and refusing other versions, keeps a wrong song out of the library, at the cost of missing some right ones. Waiting until Soulseek is done with a song keeps YouTube's lossy audio a last resort, and the setting being off by default keeps it an opt-in to sending search words to YouTube. Songs already wanted are included so a playlist imported before needn't be imported again.
