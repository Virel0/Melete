Decision: Make room suggests titles to free space, worked out from the database when asked. A
title is scored up for never being watched (35), not lately (10 after half a year, 25 after a
year), sitting untouched over a year (15), a TMDB score under 6 on 50 votes or more (20) and a
copy over 10 GB an hour (15), and down for a score of 7.5 or more (15) or a major studio among
its companies (15); from 25 it's suggested. A title worth keeping (watched by someone, a major
studio's, or rated 7 or more) is compressed when its copy is H.264 or older at 5 GB an hour or
more (half saved), else swapped for a release at about 3 GB an hour when its copy is large, else
not suggested; the rest are deleted. Never suggested: anything someone is partway through, has
on their list, or requested in the last 90 days. Major studios are a built-in list matched by
name against the companies TMDB gives with a title's details, which an admin can add to.

Why: Space runs out, and what to remove is a judgement the household makes; the server can rank
the obvious candidates and say why, and prefer shrinking what's still wanted to losing it. Major
studios' titles were chosen to be kept longer.
