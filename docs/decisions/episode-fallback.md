Decision: Amends decision 129. When Search all missing searches for a missing season and grabs nothing from it (no accepted pack holds a missing episode, as for a season still airing), it searches next for each of that season's missing episodes on its own: aired by TMDB's list, not left out on its own, with no file on disk in any folder known by the title's ids and nothing on its way. These searches are paced like the rest of the run, count in its total, and each grabs as an episode search does; the run notes it ("Title, season 2: no pack was grabbed; searching for episodes 22, 23 on their own"). Only while downloads are on. Each episode is checked again just before its turn. A season search still accepts only packs (decision 29).

Why: A season still airing has no pack, and a season search turns single episodes down, so a season partly on disk never got its newer episodes from a run. Searching them one by one only when the season search found nothing to take costs a search per missing episode, and only where it's needed.
