Decision: Private listening on the web sends no listen at all while it's on: the listen under
way when it's switched on ends there, as stopped, and once it's off recording starts again with
the next song, not the one half heard in private. It lasts for the tab and ends on signing out,
and the bar shows it while it's on. The activity tag is kept by the browser and goes on each
listen until it's taken off.

Why: The Aoide desktop app's rules, so private means the same on every device; a song started
in private and counted from its middle would say less than nothing. Shown in the bar because
something that silently stops your history must never be on unnoticed.
