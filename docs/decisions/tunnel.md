Decision: Melete can terminate WireGuard itself, in userspace, for people outside the household: GotaTun (Mullvad's maintained fork of BoringTun, MPL-2.0) on one UDP port (`MELETE_TUNNEL_PORT`, off unless set), and smoltcp as the TCP/IP stack behind it, with no TUN device and no extra container privileges. Decrypted traffic reaches only Melete's own HTTP API, at Melete's first address in the tunnel's range (`MELETE_TUNNEL_NET`, `10.73.0.0/24` by default) on port 80: a packet for any other address, port or protocol, or a fragment, is dropped before the stack, the stack has no routes, and what it sends goes only to the peer it's for, so nothing is forwarded to the home network, the host or the internet. Each peer has a fixed address, the only one WireGuard takes its packets from; requests reach the API with it as their address, through the ways in like any other, and before that the tunnel's own gate refuses a peer it no longer has and holds a watch party guest's peer to `Part::Party`, whatever the ways say. Melete's key comes from the OS's randomness and is kept sealed as other secrets are; each peer's keys and preshared key are made the same way, its private key handed once in its config to whoever made it and never kept. A device in a party makes a guest's peer with an invite (`POST /api/v1/watch-parties/{id}/tunnel-invites`); the peer lasts while the invite is unused and good, then while the guest who joined through it is in the party, checked every two seconds, and a removed peer is gone from WireGuard and its connections dropped at once. Peers live in memory, so a restart ends them. Resources are bounded: 250 peers, 32 connections (8 per peer) with buffers of a set size, bounded queues that drop when full, a minute's timeout on a silent connection, and GotaTun's handshake rate limit with cookies under load.

Why: A guest should be able to join from outside without the household opening its router to WireGuard, giving the container NET_ADMIN, or handing out access to its own network. Terminating WireGuard inside Melete and serving nothing but Melete's router keeps a guest's reach to their party alone, with the same address-based ways in as every other way. GotaTun is maintained, published as a library, and generic over its packet source, which lets a userspace stack stand in for a TUN device; its licence fits Melete's. The apps may later embed WireGuard to reach Melete the same way.
