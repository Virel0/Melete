Decision: The renderer makes a tall (9:16, 1080 by 1920) loop beside each square motion cover,
in the same job and the same pass, for every cover it takes: the cover large and sharp, a
little above the middle (moving as the square loop does when there is one), over a blurred,
darkened copy of itself that drifts slowly. Albums give it as `motion_cover_tall`. A cover's
colours also carry its palette, its main colours, each at least 3% of it and clearly apart. The
square loop's camera now drifts side to side (and a little up and down) while moving slowly in
and out, rather than going round a circle, and the loop's version is 3. This replaces the part
of an earlier decision saying Melete makes no separate tall video.

Why: Full-screen players on phones stretched the square cover to a portrait screen, and no
music service publishes full-screen player art to fetch. A background made from the cover works
for any cover, so flat and typographic covers get one too; making both in one pass costs a few
seconds a cover. The palette lets an app draw a gradient where there's no tall loop instead of
stretching the art. Moving in and out over the depth makes nearer things grow more than farther
ones, which reads as depth more clearly than the circle did.
