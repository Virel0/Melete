Decision: Amends the Soulseek-again and songs-from-YouTube decisions. A wanted song with no Soulseek search on record, wanted at least an hour, nothing of it on its way, and not in the library, is searched for by the Soulseek-again run, 60 a run at most besides the 10 searches again, counting as a search again (once a day, three times). A wanted song counts as in the library when a track of its recording is, or exactly one track's title and artist are its own (as YouTube's matching compares them) with a length within 10 seconds when both are known; Soulseek and YouTube fetch nothing for such a song, and its playlist items take that track, unless a failed download placed it. A playlist's pending song whose approved request's song failed everywhere, or that is no longer wanted, shows as `not_found` with why. Migration 0164 forgets songs' failed and nothing-fitting YouTube tries and every Soulseek search again, so what was given up on is tried again.

Why: Retries only looked at songs with a search on record, and searches are kept 30 days, so a song whose first search never ran, or ran long ago, was never looked for again while its playlist said it was on its way. A song the library had under another recording, or untagged, never filled its playlist items and could be fetched twice. Songs given up on before matching improved stayed given up on for a day or a month.
