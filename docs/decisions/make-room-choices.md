Decision: Each Make room suggestion lists every action its title can take (`options`), the suggested one first: deleting always; swapping when the title is on the wanted list and a 1080p HEVC release of its length (about 3 GB an hour) would be smaller; compressing when its copy is H.264 or older and large enough to compress well. The web offers the others beside the suggested one, acts on several ticked titles at once (each action for the ticked titles that can take it, or what's suggested for each), sorts by title, suggested action, size or what it frees, and draws the list again in place after acting, keeping its order and what's ticked.

Why: The suggestion is a judgement; an admin may rather keep a title suggested for deleting at a smaller size, and freeing space usually means acting on many titles. Sending the page back to the top after each action lost the admin's place in a long list.
