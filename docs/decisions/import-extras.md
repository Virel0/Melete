Decision: A download's extras go in the movie's or season's `Extras` folder and aren't checked
against the title's running time. A file is an extra when it's in an extras folder (those a
library scan knows, and NC, NCOP, NCED, Creditless, Menus, PV, CM, Bonus) or a word of its name
marks it (NCOP, NCED, NC OP/ED, Creditless, PV, CM, Menu, Trailer, Teaser, Preview, alone or with
a number, or a scan's `-trailer`-style ending); never when its name gives a season and episode
or a date, and never a movie's largest video. A download with only extras is refused.

Why: Packs, anime ones above all, carry creditless songs and promos without episode numbers,
and the check rightly holds back an unnumbered video it can't place; set apart, extras can't
be taken for the title, and every episode is still checked.
