Decision: The web player's equaliser, compressor, output device and visualizer are off or the
system's until chosen (the visualizer on), kept by each browser, and count at once. The output
device is offered only where the browser lets a page route Web Audio to one and names its
devices without the page asking for the microphone; Melete never asks for it. Pauses fade out
and resumes fade in over 150 ms. The visualizer sits beside Now playing in the queue panel
until a full-screen Now Playing exists, and never draws when the device asks for less motion.

Why: The same extras as the Aoide desktop app, with its presets and defaults. Asking for the
microphone only to learn the speakers' names would be a permission the music player has no
other use for.
