Decision: Melete no longer makes motion covers from still covers. The renderer, its image and
compose file, the setting, each user's choice, the task and their routes are removed; the
videos made are deleted from the config folder on the first start and their records dropped. An
album's own motion cover, from its folder, is still served as before. This replaces the
decisions that added made motion covers and raised their size.

Why: Covers made by artists move parts of the picture by hand; moving a still cover by its depth
either moved the whole cover, which drew the eye, or moved so little it wasn't worth the space,
and every cover was a few megabytes kept for something few people looked at.
