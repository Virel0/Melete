Decision: Artist results in searches for music to request get a portrait only with `MELETE_ARTIST_PORTRAITS` on (off by default, and only while MusicBrainz is on): the artist's MusicBrainz url relations give its Wikidata item (asked through the MusicBrainz client, at its one request a second), the item's image (P18, its preferred statement before a normal one, never a deprecated one) is the portrait, and Commons gives that file at 500 pixels wide with its author, licence and page, which are kept with it and given with it as `poster_credit` (the web shows them under the picture). A search looks up at most three artists it doesn't know yet, closest first, during the search; what it found is kept for 30 days and that there's none for a week, so later searches ask nothing, and a failed lookup is kept nowhere. The picture is fetched once from upload.wikimedia.org by the portrait route when first asked for, and cached; when that fails, or isn't an image, it's believed for a day: not asked for again, nor offered. On discovery posters' terms, only artists a search listed are served, for 30 days after they were last listed, 2000 at most, with housekeeping forgetting the rest and their files, and every one when the setting is off. An artist only in libraries hidden from a user is, to them, like any other artist: it's offered, listed and served the same way, so nothing tells them it's there. What leaves the server: the artist's MusicBrainz id (to MusicBrainz), its Wikidata item's id (to www.wikidata.org) and the image's file name (to commons.wikimedia.org and upload.wikimedia.org), over https to those hosts only, redirects included, with Melete's usual User-Agent and no contact details, though Wikimedia's policy asks for them.

Why: MusicBrainz keeps no artist photos, and Commons is free to use with credit. Looking up only a few artists per search keeps a search from waiting on many MusicBrainz requests at one a second; keeping a "none" keeps search from asking again about artists without a picture, and remembering a failed picture keeps every view from asking again. Answering a hidden artist differently from any other would tell a limited user what a hidden library holds. Ids and a file name say nothing about the household, and leaving out a contact keeps requests from naming it.
