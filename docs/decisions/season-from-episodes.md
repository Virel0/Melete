Decision: Amends decisions 29 and 325. A season's search still accepts only packs, but when it grabs none, the single episodes of that season it found whose only refusal is that they're single episodes are taken for the season's missing episodes instead: those aired by TMDB's list and monitored, with no file on disk and nothing on its way (as a pack taken in part finds them). For each, the best by quality rank, score and seeders is grabbed when the whole of it is still missing and it's grabbable as its episode (`grab_kind` with its first episode as the target), and it's recorded as that episode's download (its first, for one of several), brought home and checked as an episode search's is. No further search is made for them, wherever the season was searched (the title page's Search, an approved request, Search all missing, searching again after a wrong file); Search all missing's own episode searches then find them on their way. The title page's results say such a release is taken for its episode when no pack is.

Why: Some seasons exist on indexers only as single episodes, so a season's search found all of them and took none, and only Search all missing went on to search each episode again on its own. The season's own results already hold what's needed, judged by the same profile, and nothing on disk needs comparing for a missing episode.
