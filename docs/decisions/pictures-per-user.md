Decision: Amends decisions 111, 303 and 322. A picture of something not in the library (a discovery poster, a release group's cover, an artist's portrait by its MusicBrainz id) is served only to a user a search or discovery answer listed it for in the last 30 days (`picture_listings`, by kind, key and user); anyone else gets 404, as for a picture never listed. A library artist's portrait by their library id is unchanged (served to those who may see the artist). For a user who may see everything, the artwork route likewise serves another user's request's poster only to its requester (and admins). What a search shows depends only on that user's own history and public data: a cover or portrait missing or failing when one user asked isn't hidden from another's results, and of the artists a search lists, those not listed for that user lately each take one of its few lookups, known or not, so what other searches made known never shows. The pictures kept of each kind stay at most 2000: a listing that takes a kind past it wakes the forgetting at once (oldest first, with their files), besides housekeeping; who a picture was listed for is forgotten with it, after 30 days, or with the user.

Why: Served to anyone once anyone's search had listed it, a picture's route told one member of the household, by 200 or 404, what another had searched for lately. Forgetting only at housekeeping let scripted searches make Melete fetch and keep any number of pictures until the next scan; a picture forgotten while it was being fetched isn't kept, so no file outlives what remembers it.
