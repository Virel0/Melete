Decision: Each kind of image can be asked for at more sizes than answers give it at: posters at
w342, w500 or w780; backdrops at w1280 or original; stills at w300 or original; logos at w500 or
original; people's pictures at w185 or h632. Answers keep their sizes, and an app swaps the size
in the URL for a larger screen. TMDB's `original` is fetched as it is (TMDB keeps backdrops and
stills at most 3840 wide); a title's own image is made at most 3840 wide. Continue Watching and
Next Up items carry the title's backdrop and its focus.

Why: Apps on a 4K TV drew posters, stills, backdrops, logos and people's pictures 1.3 to 3 times
larger than the sizes given, and a movie in Continue Watching had only a poster for a 16:9
tile. Larger sizes only when asked keeps the cache to what's shown.
