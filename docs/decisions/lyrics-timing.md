Decision: The web UI's lyrics timing (synced lines nudged earlier or later, a quarter of a second
at a time, up to five seconds either way) is kept by the browser, for every song, and moves both
the lit line and where a line seeks to. Nothing about it is sent to the server.

Why: An offset makes up for how one device plays as much as for one file's times, so it belongs
to the device, as it does in the apps; keeping it in the browser needs no route or setting, and
the lit line and the seek have to agree or pressing a line would land a line off.
