Decision: The ways in keep going by the connection's own address, the proxy's, when a trusted proxy says who the client is (a PROXY header or `X-Forwarded-For`, which stay for sign-in limits and the log alone), so everyone a relay passes on gets what its way opens. A way in that takes in a trusted proxy's address and opens any of video, music, requests or admin is a startup warning and a note under System in admin health (`proxy_way_opens_household`), naming the way, the proxy and what the way opens.

Why: A relay is there to bring people in from anywhere; going by the client's claimed address would make what a way opens depend on what the proxy writes, and a proxy's word is only as good as the proxy. The note catches the one mistake this allows: a relay meant for guests that opens the household's parts to the whole internet.
