Decision: The web player plays music on two decks through Web Audio, so the next song is loaded
before it's wanted and starts as one ends, or fades in over it. Crossfade and Keep albums whole
follow the Aoide apps' rules and defaults and are kept by each browser. A song handed over to
ends its listen as heard to the end, and the next one's listen opens when it becomes the one
playing, so a fade never puts two open listens in the history.

Why: One audio element left a gap at every track, which breaks albums made to run on. The
apps already settled when to fade and for how long, and where to count a listen in a fade;
the web follows them so a household hears and counts the same on every device.
