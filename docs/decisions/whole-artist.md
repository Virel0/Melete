Decision: A whole artist, asked for or followed, wants every release group MusicBrainz lists
under them but spoken word (interview, audiobook, audio drama, spoken word) and broadcasts. A
single or EP wanted that way is compared, before anything is searched for, with the artist's
albums' songs (each release group's first 25 releases): when every song is on an album, by
recording or by title with only an edit, version or remaster note set aside, it's left out
(unmonitored, and stays so when the artist is asked for again). A song of its own keeps it
wanted whole. Releases asked for one by one are never compared.

Why: Asking for an artist means all their music, which for many artists is mostly singles and
EPs; but a single is often only an album's song, and wanting it would bring the song in twice.
