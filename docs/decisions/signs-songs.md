Decision: A subtitle track counts as forced when the file flags it, or when its title names it
the signs and/or songs track ("Signs & Songs", "Signs/Songs", "S&S", "Signs") and doesn't also
say it's full or dialogue; a subtitle file beside the video counts when its name says `.forced.`
or `.signs.`. It's decided when tracks are read, so files already probed follow without a
rescan.

Why: Anime releases mark their signs and songs track by name rather than by the forced flag, so
with dubbed audio the track that translates only on-screen text was never picked.
